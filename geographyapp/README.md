# Geo Quest

A separate Grade 7 geography practice app with:

- Ten 15-question sets for **The Story of Indian Farming**
- Three 10-question sets for **India and Her Neighbours**

Correct answer positions are mixed across choices, and incorrect answers include a hint explaining the answer.

Every farming question includes a topic-matched original illustration instead of the India map. Questions in the other chapters include an original schematic map of India and neighbouring regions, with the northern and western areas shown. Map-challenge questions highlight their numbered location. The India Map Challenge has four 10-question sets covering physical features, neighbouring countries, directions, and surrounding regions.

The farming questions are original practice material based only on [The Story of Indian Farming](https://smartachievers.online/public/study_materials/gees201.pdf). The other questions are original practice material based on their selected chapter topics.

## Run on your phone

1. Install Node.js 22.13 or newer.
2. In this folder, run `npm install`.
3. Run `npx expo start`.
4. Open Expo Go and scan the QR code.
